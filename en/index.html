what whit does

How does whit help me find out what does me good?

whit connects your mood with what makes up your days: sleep, movement, people, places and your habits. After a few weeks you see what lines up with your good days and with your hard ones.

For every habit, whit shows how you feel on days with it and on days without it. Add trends for mood and energy and reviews for the week, month and year. That way you spot patterns and can do more of what does you good, on purpose.

What is a whit, and how does it work?

A whit is an entry in the app: a quick look at how you feel right now. You pick your mood and your energy, and a blob takes on their shape and colour.

If you like, you add sleep or movement, people, places and a note. A whit takes just a few seconds, as often a day as you want.

Is whit a habit tracker too?

Yes. You tick off habits with one swipe, set weekly goals and keep streaks, with one freeze per week.

The difference: whit shows you whether a habit really does you good, instead of only counting how often you do it.

How is whit different from other mood trackers?

Most mood trackers collect entries. whit connects them: it shows you how sleep, movement, people, places and habits relate to your mood, so you can actually get better.

And whit stays light: a whit is logged in seconds, and everything is designed to make sense without a manual. On top of that, whit is completely free, works without an account and is end-to-end encrypted.

Are there widgets and reminders?

Yes, and you can make both your own. Widgets come in many kinds and sizes for your home screen, from your mood to your streak.

You set reminders freely: for your whits, your habits and your reviews.

cost & ads

Yes. Every feature is free: no Pro version, no subscription, no in-app purchases and nothing behind a paywall. That is not going to change.

Are there ads in whit?

No. whit shows no ads and contains no advertising IDs, no trackers and no usage analytics.

How can whit be free and still this private?

whit is a personal project, with no investors and no business model. It has nothing to sell, data included. That is why whit can do without ads, tracking and subscriptions, and your entries stay with you.

privacy & security

Do I need an account?

No. whit works fully offline and without an account. Then not a single entry leaves your phone.

You only need a free account for the optional cloud backup and for syncing between devices.

How secure is the cloud backup?

Your entries, notes, habits, name and settings are encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM before they are uploaded. The cloud only holds unreadable ciphertext, and only you have the key.

Only the technical details that signing in needs stay readable: your email address, how many documents your account has and when they were stored. Document names are random.

What happens if I lose my recovery code?

Then the cloud copy can no longer be decrypted. That is exactly what keeps it private: nobody else has the key.

The data on your phone is not affected, and you can replace the cloud copy with it at any time. Keep the code in your password manager.

What data does whit use?

whit collects no data about you. It only works with what you note yourself, and that stays with you. No location, no contacts, no photos, no usage statistics.

Crash reports are off until you switch them on in settings, and they never contain your entries.

Can I lock whit with my fingerprint?

Yes. The app lock asks for your fingerprint, face or device PIN. Your operating system does the check; whit only gets back "unlocked" or "not unlocked".

your data

Can I export all my data?

Yes, at any time and in full, under Settings → Data: as JSON, which you can import again on another device, or as CSV.

How do I delete my data or my account?

Delete single whits in your history. Everything at once under Settings → Data → "Clear all data", on the device and in the cloud. The account and all its cloud data with "Delete account".

Without the app, an email to gobi275@proton.me is enough, and everything is deleted within 30 days. Deleting means deleting.

Can I use whit on several devices or move to a new phone?

Yes. With the optional account, whit syncs end-to-end encrypted. On the new phone you sign in and enter your recovery code.

Without an account, you move with an export and import.

Is whit a medical product?

No. whit is not a certified medical device and makes no diagnoses. It helps you get to know yourself and your everyday life better.

If you feel low for a longer time, a doctor or a counselling service is the right place to turn to.

What does the name whit mean?

In English, a whit is the smallest possible amount, as in "not worth a whit". Every entry in the app is a whit: a tiny data point on its own, and over months an honest picture of how you live.
